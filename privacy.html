What this site collects

You can read the pages without creating an account. The site does not ask for your name, email address or payment details, and it does not sell personal information.

The site uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-HFEL864LGT) and may show advertisements from Google. Google sets cookies and similar technologies on your device. Those are used to understand how the site is used, to remember your ad preferences, to serve ads, and to measure whether an ad was seen or clicked. Google may receive your IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and an advertising identifier.

Cookies

A cookie is a small file stored on your device. Google Analytics uses cookies such as _ga to tell one visit from another. If ads are shown, Google may also use advertising cookies. You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser. If you do, some measurement and ads may not work as intended. The pages themselves will still load.

Advertising

Google, and advertisers working through Google, may use data collected on this site and on other sites to show you ads. You can control personalised ads at Google’s Ads Settings, and you can opt out of some cookies at Your Online Choices. Google’s own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy and its advertising technologies are described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

How long data is kept

Analytics and advertising data are kept by Google under Google’s retention settings. This site does not keep its own database of visitors.

Your rights

If you are in the UK, you can ask what personal data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to it being used for advertising. Because the data sits with Google, the practical route is Google’s tools above. For anything about this site itself, write via casualfootballshirts.co.uk.
