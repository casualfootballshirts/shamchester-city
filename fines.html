The invented tariff

How the satire is scored

British spelling. Pounds sterling. Interest compounds daily at 4% a year from 1 July after each season until the day you open this page. Seasons: every Premier League campaign Manchester City have played from 2008–09 through 2017–18.

Finished below City

Every club that finished below Manchester City that season: £3m, plus interest from the following 1 July.

Pushed in Europe

Remove City from the table and shift clubs up. If a club would have made the Champions League but only got Europa League: £20m CL revenue + £10m receipts + £10m sponsors. If a club would have made CL or EL but missed Europe entirely: the same £40m, labelled for the competition they would have made. Only when City occupied the relevant European place; only clubs whose status actually changes.

Lost the title

When City won the league, the runner-up already has the £3m “finished below” line. On top: £10m sponsor bonus + £10m boosted earnings. Manchester United or Liverpool only, when second to a City title: a further £20m for reputational damage.

Cup final

When City won the FA Cup or League Cup, only the club they beat in that final: £5m boosted revenue. Not every round.
