The rules

How the total is scored

Interest compounds daily at 4% a year from 1 July after each season until the day you open this page. Only Premier League seasons from 2008–09 through 2017–18 are included. The pound amounts are an educated calculation of lost earnings from football accounts.

Finished below City

Any club that finished below Manchester City is given £3 million for the league place they lost, with interest from 1 July after that season.

Missed Europe

If City are taken out of the table, a club that would have reached the Champions League instead of the Europa League is given £40 million. That is £20 million in European revenue, £10 million in match receipts and £10 million in sponsorship. A club that would have been in Europe and missed out entirely gets the same £40 million. Only clubs whose European place actually changes are included.

Lost the title

In a season City won the league, the runners-up get £10 million for the sponsor bonus and £10 million for the extra earnings that come with winning it. The £3 million for finishing below City is already counted. Manchester United and Liverpool get a further £20 million in any season they finished second to City.

Cup final

When City won the FA Cup or the League Cup, the club they beat in the final is given £5 million for the earnings a cup win brings. Earlier rounds are not included. Sunderland in 2013–14 and Liverpool in 2015–16 also get £20 million because losing that League Cup final meant they missed Europe the next season. Stoke in 2010–11 and Arsenal in 2017–18 already had a route into Europe, so they do not.
